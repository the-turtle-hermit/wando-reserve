Meetings & Notices

Official community communications are coordinated through the Association and its property management company.

Annual membership meeting

Homeowners are encouraged to attend, ask questions, participate in elections and discuss finances, maintenance and community improvements. The bylaws provide for a November meeting or another date established by the Board. Meetings are commonly held near year-end.

Official annual meeting notices are distributed by mail in accordance with the governing documents; email may supplement those notices. Follow the official notice for meeting access, dates, voting and proxy instructions.

Projects and community notices

Tree work, landscaping changes, infrastructure projects, service interruptions and community-wide compliance deadlines are communicated by management through email or other appropriate official channels. Individual compliance notices are sent privately.

This website is a permanent reference, not a live calendar or notice board.

Keep your contact information current

If you are not receiving Association emails, sign into the homeowner portal and verify your email, mailing address and telephone number. Owners should share relevant notices and standards with tenants.

Community input

Suggestions for beautification, safety, landscaping and maintenance are welcome. Contact management to share ideas with the Board. Projects are evaluated against available funds, governing documents and community priorities.
