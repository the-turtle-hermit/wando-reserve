Common tasks

Where should I go?

I need to pay dues or check my account.
Use the CMG homeowner portal for account-specific and private management functions.
I want to make an exterior change.
Review the Community Standards and governing documents first, then submit an ARC / ARB request when approval is required.
I see a community maintenance or compliance issue.
Use management or Board channels. Photos and a clear date/location are helpful. Avoid confrontation with neighbors.
I need a building permit or Town inspection.
Contact the Town of Mount Pleasant Building Inspections Division at 843-884-5184. HOA approval does not replace municipal permitting requirements.
