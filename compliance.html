Community Care & Compliance

Learn how community concerns are handled and how to report maintenance or compliance issues privately.

How the Board approaches concerns

Reasonable first. Enforcement when needed.

The goal is a clean, safe and attractive community, not constant enforcement. Most issues should be handled through clear communication and a reasonable opportunity to correct the condition based on the nature of the violation.

Fines may be assessed when violations remain unresolved or when circumstances warrant enforcement under the Association's rules and governing documents.

When reporting a concern

  • Note the date and approximate location.
  • Describe the issue clearly and factually.
  • Include photographs when they can be taken safely.
  • Do not confront the resident involved.
  • Send identifying information privately to management or the Board.

Need to report something?

Use the CMG portal or another approved private Association contact method for address-specific concerns and evidence.
